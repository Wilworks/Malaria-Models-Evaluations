# Scientific Inquiry & Research Pipeline

Use this skill whenever conducting empirical evaluations, scientific benchmarks, or academic paper drafting with Wilfred Ayine Asumboya.

## Core Philosophy
1. **Zero Synthetic Bloat:** No hardcoded insight strings, fake progress bars, or decorative console gimmicks. All outputs must derive from authentic tensor passes on real disk files.
2. **Intellectual Sovereignty:** Never do the researcher's thinking for them. The researcher (Wilfred) is the Principal Investigator; the assistant is the computational engine.
3. **Defensibility:** The researcher must be able to defend every claim, equation, and figure independently to their academic supervisors.

---

## The 4-Stage Operational Protocol

### Stage 1: Hypothesis & Skeletal Scaffolding
Before running full computations or drafting discussions:
1. Define explicit, numbered **Research Questions (RQs)** with clear clinical/technical hypotheses.
2. Generate a **Skeletal Paper Scaffold** in LaTeX (`main.tex`) and Markdown:
   - Complete front matter (Author, PI, Affiliations, Keywords).
   - Introduction setting the clinical context and listing the RQs.
   - Comprehensive Methodology detailing datasets, hardware, models, and statistical tests.
   - Empty Result Tables (`tab:thick`, `tab:thin`, `tab:stratified`) with column headers ready to receive data.
   - Discussion placeholders labeled with `\userinput{Wilfred's Section: [Specific guided questions]}`.

### Stage 2: Authentic Empirical Execution & Data Hygiene
1. **Pre-Inference Data Audit:**
   - Scan all raw image/sensor files for byte truncation, missing EOF markers, or corrupted headers.
   - Quarantine defective instances into `data/quarantine_corrupted/`.
   - Document the quarantine count and percentage in the methodology section.
2. **Uncompromised Inference:**
   - Execute real forward passes on all valid instances.
   - Do not truncate or take shortcuts unless explicitly requested for quick testing.
   - Record raw predictions with confidence, bounding boxes, and ground truth in a structured CSV manifest.
3. **Statistical Derivation:**
   - Compute Sensitivity, Specificity, PPV, NPV, and F1-score.
   - Calculate exact two-sided 95% Wilson Score Confidence Intervals.
   - Populate the empty result tables with the true empirical numbers.

### Stage 3: Human Plain-English Interpretation
**STOP HERE.** Do not write the Discussion section yet.
1. Present the populated result tables to Wilfred.
2. Pose the guided discussion prompts corresponding to each Research Question:
   - *"What does this difference in specificity mean clinically?"*
   - *"Why did Model A collapse while Model B remained stable?"*
   - *"What physical differences in slide preparation or optics explain these center-level variations?"*
3. **Wait for Wilfred's explanation in his own words.**

### Stage 4: Publication Synthesis
1. Take Wilfred's direct reasoning, clinical intuitions, and real-world domain knowledge.
2. Translate his exact thoughts into scholarly, rigorous academic prose for the Discussion, Study Limitations, and Policy Recommendations.
3. Ensure every argument traces directly back to Wilfred's insight and the empirical numbers in the tables.
